Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Kapuch Clips ("the App") is a desktop tool that uploads videos from the user's computer to the user's own TikTok account via the official TikTok API.

1. Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) — to show which account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens — to upload videos on your behalf.
  • Video files and captions you choose to upload.

2. How data is stored

All data, including tokens, is stored only locally on the user's own computer. We do not operate servers that collect, store or process your data, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties. Videos are sent only directly to TikTok.

3. Data retention and deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the App's local files, and revoke the App's access in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

4. Children

The App is not intended for users under 18.

5. Changes

We may update this Policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
